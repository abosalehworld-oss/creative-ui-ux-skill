# Changelog

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-19 — Integrity & Freshness Hardening

### Added
- `core/00_agent_integrity_protocol.md` — binding protocol read before anything else: 10 Unbreakable Rules,
  Simple-Execution Mode for smaller models, real-date anchoring, execution-mode honesty (no fake research when
  a model has no web tools), evidence labels, prompt-injection defense, and a mandatory **Final Self-Audit**;
  plus a UI/UX addendum (no invented stars/sizes/metrics, no fake PNG/ICO deliverables, computed contrast,
  license & trademark discipline).
- `references/freshness_registry.md` — dated facts with official sources (WCAG 2.2, INP, Motion/HeroUI renames, etc.).
- Critical Rules 9–12: Accessibility as release blocker, Licenses & Trademarks, No Fake Deliverables, Final Self-Audit.
- Binding contract block at the top of `SKILL.md`; protocol added as step 0 in every Mode's reading map.

### Changed
- `GEMINI.md` rewritten with the 10 rules inline.
- Hardcoded years ("2025 2026") in search queries replaced by the real current year.
- Banned-library rules apply to new code; existing projects are not rewritten without client approval.
- Motion guidance: Framer Motion → `motion`, `@motionone/dom` and AOS no longer recommended, sizes must be measured.
- Performance targets: FID → INP; app-size and fps claims must be measured, not assumed.
- WordPress/Shopify: no edits to parent-theme files, theme duplication first, self-hosted fonts preferred.
- Font names corrected (IBM Plex Sans Arabic); Fontshare license note; system fonts allowed on native platforms.
